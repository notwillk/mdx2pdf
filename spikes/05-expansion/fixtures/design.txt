Design fixtures: scope, containers, grafting, recursion, and the round trip. Expected
results were written before the first run. Don't edit them to match a result: a surprise
is a finding. "--- expect-md" compares events, so it means the same document, not the same
text. "--- expect-error" lists text the error must contain, and "!" text it must not.

Corrected after the first run, whose results stay in results/first-run/: paragraph-then-grafted-
paragraph-stay-apart expected one paragraph, but a tag alone on a line interrupts a paragraph,
so its three blocks are right. Everything from same-component-nested-in-its-own-children on was added
after that run, with its result written before it first ran. The expression-alone-on-a-line
fixtures come from a probe that found a real problem: see the findings.
